# Patterns

A nightly reflection app that uses Claude to ask contextual questions about your day, so you can build a meaningful log and track trends in what affects your sleep, HRV, heart rate, and other Oura ring metrics.

## Why

Oura shows *what* your sleep and readiness scores are, but not *why*. The app's built-in tagging feature is hard to find and easy to forget, so most days go unlogged. Patterns replaces tagging with a short, journal-style check-in at bedtime:

1. You wind down for bed and open Patterns.
2. Claude asks a few questions shaped by your day and your recent scores.
3. Your answers become a log you can compare against your Oura data over time.

The goal is to spot correlations between lifestyle factors and how you feel the next day.

## Who it's for

Biohackers who check their Oura stats every day and want to learn which habits help and which hurt. The first user is the author.

## Open questions

- How much data needs to be filtered in to make the questions useful.
- Whether Claude's suggested questions actually help surface correlations.
