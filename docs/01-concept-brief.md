1. Working Name: 

Patterns

2. Pitch: 

A nightly reflection app that uses Claude to ask contextual questions about your day, so you can build a meaningful log and track trends of what affects your sleep, HRV, heart rate, etc.

3. Who it's for:

Me, Ava, a biohacker who religiously checks my oura ring statistics to understand what lifestyle factors work for me and what negatively affects my Oura scores. 

4. The job it does: 

When I'm winding down for bed, I want to quickly capture what happened in the context of my sleep and readiness scores, so I can identify correlations between my lifestyle and how I feel the next day. 

5. What people do instead today: 

There's a tagging feature on the Oura ring app, but its hard to find, and I forget it exists. There's a lot of friction and I think it would be easier to do it journal style. 

6. Biggest unknown: 

How much I have to filter in, and whether or not Claude's suggested questions can actually help spot correlations.
